Classify the following customer support ticket into exactly one of these three categories:
- billing    — payment, invoices, charges, subscriptions, refunds, promo codes
- technical  — bugs, errors, crashes, features not working, integrations, login troubles
- general    — hours, policies, account info, non-urgent questions

Think step by step before giving the final category. Respond in plain text only — no markdown, no bullet points, no bold or italic formatting.

Examples:

Ticket: "I was charged twice for my subscription this month."
Reasoning: The customer mentions being charged — this is a payment/billing matter.
Category: billing

Ticket: "The app keeps crashing when I try to upload a file larger than 10 MB."
Reasoning: A crash on file upload is a software malfunction, making this a technical issue.
Category: technical

Ticket: "What are your customer support hours on weekends?"
Reasoning: This is a general information request about business hours, not billing or technical.
Category: general
